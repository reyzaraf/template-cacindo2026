Tata Kelola KAKIKAKI Governance

KAKI dijalankan oleh Task Force yang menangani pekerjaan harian, didampingi Advisory Committee yang terdiri dari perwakilan asosiasi bisnis nasional dan pakar antikorupsi. KAKI is run by a Task Force that handles day-to-day work, supported by an Advisory Committee made up of representatives from national business associations and anti-corruption experts.
